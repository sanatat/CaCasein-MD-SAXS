# Accurate project description for a PhD application

**Project:** CaCasein-MD-SAXS — Sequence-informed coarse-grained MD and synthetic SAXS robustness study (2026)

Built and tested an exploratory reduced-unit Langevin dynamics model of beta-casein-inspired chain assemblies with mobile Ca-like beads. Computed synthetic Debye SAXS profiles, bead-contact and cross-chain bridging metrics, and reproducibility/initialization controls (21 independent simulations across two time-length settings). Identified strong sensitivity of apparent ion binding to starting configurations, preventing premature equilibrium or experimental-validation claims. Python, NumPy, Numba, SciPy, Matplotlib, pytest.

This is **not** a Martini 3 force-field simulation or a validated biological model; no empirical SAXS fit or quantitative Ca affinity was produced.
